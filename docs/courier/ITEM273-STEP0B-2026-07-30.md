## FIX 1 — Owner-slot PII hardening

Site: `supabase/functions/_shared/ltp/section-composers/cppa-risk.ts`
(`sanitizeRoleTitleSegments`, `stripParentheticals`, `hasNameBigram`,
`contributorRoleTitles`, consumed by `ownerForKind`).

Order of operations per comma/semicolon/newline segment:

1. (a) strip ALL parenthetical content, including an unterminated trailing
   `(...` tail and an orphan `...)` head produced by splitting inside a
   parenthetical;
2. (d-pre) strip terminal sentence punctuation (kills the double period);
3. reject contact handles (`@`, 7+ digit runs);
4. (b) reject narrative: length > 60 chars, any of `. ! ? ; :`, or any
   closed-list token — `is, are, has, have, been, was, remains, vacant,
   following, assigned, departure, since`;
5. require a role word to be present;
6. (c) drop any segment containing two adjacent Capitalized tokens that are
   not role words (personal-name bigram);
7. (d) dedupe, join with ", ", no trailing periods.

### FIX 1(e) — harness detector `pii_owner_name` (MATERIAL)

`evaluateOwnerSlotPii` in `_shared/ltp/replay/substance-gates.ts` scans text
following an `Owner:` label inside `priority_actions` for (i) parenthesised
capitalized bigrams and (ii) the closed narrative-verb list.

**Honest limits.** This is a heuristic, not a name recogniser. It will MISS:
a single-token surname (`Owner: Trent`), an uncapitalised name, and a name
that reads as a role-shaped segment (`Officer Trent`). It will FALSE-POSITIVE
on a legitimate two-word capitalized proper noun in parentheses (e.g. an
org-unit name). It catches the observed defect shapes and nothing more.
