## 3. What was built

`supabase/functions/_shared/ltp/cppa-cyber-deliverables/`

- **`components.ts`** — the generated corpus binding. Each component carries `number`, `slug`, `label`, `citation` (always the specific subdivision, never a bare § 7123), `verbatim`, `prior_label`, and `disposition`.
- **`types.ts`** — the four-part analysis shape (`standard` → `record_fact` → `application` → `verdict`) shared with the governance and IR deliverables, plus `CyberComponentCoverage`, `EvidenceSufficiency`, `IndependenceDetermination`, `ReadinessDetermination`.
- **`build.ts`** — pure builders, single-writer, no I/O, no clock:
  - `buildComponentCoverage` — maturity rubric → statutory verdict per subdivision.
  - `buildEvidenceSufficiency` — **§ 7122(d)** applied: the statute grounds findings in "documents reviewed, sampling and testing performed, and interviews conducted". A **policy alone is an assertion, not a test**, so it yields `partial`; a log, scan, or config export yields `sufficient`; a declared absence yields a **reasoned** `insufficient`; an unmentioned component yields `unknown` rather than a guess.
  - `buildIndependenceDetermination` — § 7122 conditions, with the internal reporting-line condition marked `not_applicable` (not "passed") on external engagements.
  - `readiness_determination` — the report's conclusion, replacing the mean.

## 4. Readiness replaces the mean — and can conclude "ready"

Chapter 2's specific complaint was that the product could not say a company was ready; it could only report an average. `readiness_determination` reaches one of `ready` / `not_ready` / `record_insufficient`, names its blocking components, and cites § 7124. A thin record concludes **`record_insufficient`, never `not_ready`** — absence of evidence is not evidence of non-compliance, and conflating the two is the failure mode that a scored mean makes structurally unavoidable.

**DEMOTION LAW.** The mean is not deleted; it is demoted to `mean_score_readability_aid`, labelled as having **no statutory basis** and caveated to defer to the determination. A **SEPARATION GUARD** pin asserts the conclusion's prose never restates the mean.

**DEDUP LAW.** `_w10_cyber_aggregates.ts` `scrubAuthoredAggregates` now emits the canonical mean sentence at most once, fixing the duplication Chapter 2 recorded.
