## §R.1 — Law (verbatim from dispatch)

> Every content-bearing token in a factor `weight_note` must originate from (i) the plan's intake-ledger verbatims, (ii) the registry vocabulary (display labels, factor/gate terms), or (iii) the closed CONNECTIVE LEXICON. Violating notes are deterministically REPLACED by the grounded form: `the intake records "{ledger_verbatim}" for {field_display_label}` (or the standard "no record evidence" where no ledger row supports the factor). Replacement is mechanical, never model-mediated.

Wired between the CP-C coherence screen and validator in `runPass1Llm`.

## §R.2 — CONNECTIVE LEXICON (verbatim, panel condition 1)

Closed, curated analytic vocabulary — **not** a bare stop-word list. Any add/remove is a courier turn. The list below is the authoritative mirror of the codebase constant `CONNECTIVE_LEXICON` in `supabase/functions/_shared/ltp/pass1-grounded-note.ts`.

**Record verbs (what the intake DOES relative to the record):** states, state, stated, record, records, recorded, documents, documented, identifies, identified, reports, reported, describes, described, lists, listed, names, named, notes, noted, confirms, confirmed, provides, provided, indicates, indicated, shows, showed, supplies, supplied, attests, attested.

**Neutral analytic terms (what the RECORD is or is not):** present, absent, silent, unaddressed, unclear, documented, undocumented, unstated, missing, sufficient, insufficient, adequate, inadequate, specific, general, generic, particular, applicable, inapplicable, engaged, not-engaged.

**Absence phrasings:** does, not, no, none, without, lacks, lacked, is, are, was, were, be, been, being, has, have, had, carries, carried.

**Hedging function words:** may, might, could, would, should, likely, unlikely, appears, appear, seems, seem, suggests, suggested.

**Quantity function words** (numerals themselves ground through ledger verbatims): one, two, three, several, multiple, few, many, any, all, each, every, single, additional.

**Scope function words:** for, the, a, an, of, in, on, at, by, to, from, with, within, under, over, per, as, and, or, but, than, then, that, this, these, those, such, which, when, where, while, because, since, if, unless, only.

**Register connectives (fixed frames the replacement uses):** purpose, stated-purpose, for-the-stated-purpose-of.

**Canonical intake terms admissible as analytic vocabulary:** record, records, intake, assessment.

## §R.3 — Ledger verbatims render AS MARKED QUOTATIONS (panel condition 2)

The deterministic replacement form is:

> `the intake records "{ledger_verbatim}" for {field_display_label}`

Curly ("smart") and straight quotation marks are treated as connective punctuation by the tokenizer, never as content tokens. Template frames retain all eloquence; the ledger verbatim is quoted verbatim — this makes the constraint a legal-register feature.

## §R.4 — Normalization spec (panel condition 3)

- **Case-folding.** Lowercase before token comparison.
- **Punctuation.** Split on non-`[a-z0-9\-']`; keep intra-word hyphens and apostrophes; trim edge hyphens/apostrophes.
- **Inflection tolerance:** `token`, `token±s`, `token±es`, `token±ing`, `token±ed`, and `y↔ies`. No stemming beyond these rules.
- **Numerals:** grounded iff the exact numeric string (post case-fold) appears as a substring of any ledger verbatim. No rounding, no unit rewriting.
- **Quotations** (`" ' " " " ' ' \``): connective punctuation only.

Positive fixture (must NOT fire): `the intake records email address for the stated purpose` — every content token is either a lexicon inflection or a ledger verbatim.

Negative fixture (MUST fire, reproducing run #179): `delivers audience insights for the campaign` — `audience` + `insights` are ungrounded → note is replaced with `the intake records "{ledger_verbatim}" for {label}`.
