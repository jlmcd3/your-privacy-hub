## Verbatim (d)(1)(A)–(C) enumeration

> (d) "Business" means:
>
> (1) A sole proprietorship, partnership, limited liability company, corporation, association, or other legal entity that is organized or operated for the profit or financial benefit of its shareholders or other owners, that collects consumers' personal information, or on the behalf of which such information is collected and that alone, or jointly with others, determines the purposes and means of the processing of consumers' personal information, that does business in the State of California, and that satisfies one or more of the following thresholds:
>
> (A) As of January 1 of the calendar year, had annual gross revenues in excess of twenty-five million dollars ($25,000,000) in the preceding calendar year, as adjusted pursuant to subdivision (d) of Section 1798.199.95.
>
> (B) Alone or in combination, annually buys, sells, or shares the personal information of 100,000 or more consumers or households.
>
> (C) Derives 50 percent or more of its annual revenues from selling or sharing consumers' personal information.

Subdivision (d)(2) (affiliate/common-branding coverage) is also captured verbatim in `verbatim_excerpt`.

## CPI-adjustment nuance — criterion (A)

The $25,000,000 figure is expressly **CPI-adjusted** under Civ. Code § 1798.199.95(d). Downstream emitters and opening-paragraph copy must NOT freeze the $25M figure as a static bright line. Quote the statutory language verbatim and, where an applicability determination turns on it, anchor to § 1798.199.95(d) for the adjustment mechanism rather than asserting the un-adjusted number is dispositive for any given year.

## Criterion (B) semantic flag — for the emitter turn

Criterion (B) reads "**annually buys, sells, or shares** the personal information of **100,000 or more consumers or households**" — the threshold is on the count of **consumers or households whose PI is bought/sold/shared**, not on total records processed and not on total consumers about whom the business merely *collects* PI. Emitters that translate (B) into an applicability claim must:

- NOT paraphrase as "processes personal information of 100,000+ consumers" (over-broad; captures collectors that neither sell nor share).
- NOT paraphrase as "100,000+ records" (units mismatch; the statutory unit is consumers/households).
- Preserve the buy/sell/share verbs (all three, disjunctive) and the consumer-or-household object.

The RISK-OPENING-PARAGRAPH pilot dispatch should carry this flag into the emitter's fact-ledger check so a facts payload lacking sell/share activity cannot trip a (B)-based applicability finding.
