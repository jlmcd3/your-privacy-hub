## Verbatim (d)(1)(A)–(C) enumeration (from corpus)

> (d) "Business" means:
>
> (1) A sole proprietorship, partnership, limited liability company, corporation, association, or other legal entity that is organized or operated for the profit or financial benefit of its shareholders or other owners, that collects consumers' personal information, or on the behalf of which such information is collected and that alone, or jointly with others, determines the purposes and means of the processing of consumers' personal information, that does business in the State of California, and that satisfies one or more of the following thresholds:
>
> (A) As of January 1 of the calendar year, had annual gross revenues in excess of twenty-five million dollars ($25,000,000) in the preceding calendar year, as adjusted pursuant to subdivision (d) of Section 1798.199.95.
>
> (B) Alone or in combination, annually buys, sells, or shares the personal information of 100,000 or more consumers or households.
>
> (C) Derives 50 percent or more of its annual revenues from selling or sharing consumers' personal information.

## Operative revenue threshold — as the corpus stores it

The corpus text expresses criterion (A) as:

> "had annual gross revenues in excess of twenty-five million dollars ($25,000,000) in the preceding calendar year, **as adjusted pursuant to subdivision (d) of Section 1798.199.95**."

The T7 emitter must **use the corpus figure verbatim** and **never hard-code a numeric threshold**. CPI adjustments under § 1798.199.95(d) may mean the operative figure for a given year is not the flat $25,000,000 shown in the statute text. Emitters that surface (A) as an applicability trigger must quote the corpus string (including the "as adjusted" cross-reference) rather than substituting a static number.

## Criterion (B) semantic flag — for the T7 emitter turn

Criterion (B) reads: "**annually buys, sells, or shares** the personal information of **100,000 or more consumers or households**." The threshold is on the count of **consumers or households whose PI is bought/sold/shared**, not on:

- total records processed (unit mismatch),
- consumers about whom the business merely *collects* PI without selling/sharing (over-broad; captures pure collectors),
- households counted separately from consumers (statute counts them together in the disjunction).

Emitters must preserve the buy/sell/share verbs (all three, disjunctive) and the consumer-or-household object. The T7 fact-ledger check must reject any (B)-based applicability finding when the facts payload records no sell/share activity.
